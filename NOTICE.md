Little Metro / 小小地铁 is an independent educational reconstruction of the core transport-network gameplay of Mini Metro. Mini Metro is developed and owned by Dinosaur Polo Club. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Dinosaur Polo Club.

The maps, meshes, interface, sounds and transport simulation are newly authored here. No commercial Mini Metro or Boomerang Fu assets, source code, audio or maps are distributed. The visual direction references the friendly low-poly miniature aesthetic of Boomerang Fu by Cranky Watermelon.

The web UI bundles a WOFF2 subset of Noto Sans SC Regular, Copyright 2014-2021 Adobe, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, plus glyphs subset from DejaVu Sans (Bitstream Vera-derived license) and Noto Sans Symbols 2 (Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1) for map controls and destination symbols. License texts are included at `public/fonts/OFL.txt`, `public/fonts/DEJAVU-LICENSE.txt`, and `public/fonts/NOTO-SYMBOLS-OFL.txt`; these are independent open-source UI fonts, not original-game assets.

The HTTP controller, cancellation and SDK packaging patterns are adapted from the Hexgarden experiment in the original game-re-build research workspace.

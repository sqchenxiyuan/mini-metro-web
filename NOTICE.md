# Attribution

Little Metro / 小小地铁 is an independent educational reconstruction of the core transport-network gameplay of Mini Metro. Mini Metro is developed and owned by Dinosaur Polo Club. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Dinosaur Polo Club.

The maps, meshes, interface, sounds and transport simulation are newly authored here. No commercial Mini Metro or Boomerang Fu assets, source code, fonts, audio or maps are distributed. The visual direction references the friendly low-poly miniature aesthetic of Boomerang Fu by Cranky Watermelon.

The HTTP controller, cancellation and SDK packaging patterns are adapted from the Hexgarden experiment in the original game-re-build research workspace. Little Metro is maintained here as a standalone repository under GPL-3.0-or-later. See LICENSE.md. The rules engine does not use Hextris gameplay code.

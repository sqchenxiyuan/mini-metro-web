The maps, meshes, interface, sounds and transport simulation are newly authored here. No commercial Mini Metro or Boomerang Fu assets, source code, audio or maps are distributed. The visual direction references the friendly low-poly miniature aesthetic of Boomerang Fu by Cranky Watermelon.

The web UI bundles Noto Sans SC Regular, Copyright 2014-2021 Adobe, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Its license text is included at `public/fonts/OFL.txt`; the font is an independent open-source UI font, not an original-game asset.
